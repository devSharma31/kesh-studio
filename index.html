Before you book

Questions people ask us

Can I just walk in?

Yes, if a chair is free. But evenings and weekends fill up, so a WhatsApp booking means you won't wait.

What if I'm running late?

Send us a message. Up to 10 minutes late is fine. After that we'll try to fit you in, or move you to the next free slot.

Why is there a token for colour and keratin?

These take 1.5–2.5 hours of a stylist's time, so we hold the slot with ₹200. It comes off your bill. Cancel 2+ hours before and you get it back.

Do you use good products? Is colour safe for me?

We use professional salon brands, with an ammonia-free option. We do a free patch test 48 hours before your first colour.

I don't know what cut suits me. Can I ask first?

Of course. Pick “Not sure yet” in the booking form, or send us a photo on WhatsApp. A stylist will suggest options and the price.

Is it really unisex? Are there separate stylists?

Yes. We have men's and women's stylists, and a female stylist for any service on request.
